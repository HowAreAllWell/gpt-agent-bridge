current judgment or authority. Project instructions retain their scope; local
cognition holds this project's decisions, causes and standing grants. This skill
supplies a reusable procedure and grants no role or new authority.

`bridge_overview(project_ref)` when runtime evidence matters. Separate current
observation from dated receipts and preserve contradictions. Continue from the
current judgment, unresolved dependency and authorized next action; do not repeat
closed work merely because its evidence is historical.
